# Brief — Asiacell concept reel for allawi.psd

## Intent

v2. The user said v1 (12 s, with a 3D layer breakdown) was "meh": the start looked fake and the middle was boring. They chose the short punchy ad.

The user's stats (Metricool, 3–7 Oct 2026): reels are the only posts reaching non-followers, but only 8–25% of viewers stay past 3 seconds. So the joke and the brand land by 2.5 s, and the reel is short enough (7.5 s) to be watched to the end and replayed.

- **0–0.72s:** the real room (the CC0 bulb photo) lit, with «كل بيت عراقي / يعرف هاللحظة..». The grid flickers and drops.
- **0.72–1.4s:** black, the filament's afterglow, and «الوطنية طفت..».
- **1.38–2.2s:** «النت» (white neon) and «ما طفى.» (red neon) ignite, and their red light spills over the room. The signal bars light up, and the street generator starts.
- **2.3–6.4s:** the sub line, the logo and the button. From 4.2s, the share ask «دزّه لواحد بيتهم بلا وطنية هسه».
- **6.4–7.5s:** the power comes back and the frame returns to the opening frame, so the reel loops.

## Assets

- Room and bulb: the CC0 photo by Ashesh Magar, graded into lit, dark and red-lit plates by `photo.py`.
- Asiacell logo from asiacell.com (brand red #e82228).
- SFX: synthesized by `sfx.py`, plus the user's own kit (boom, whoosh, pops). No music.
