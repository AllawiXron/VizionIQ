# Brief — Asiacell concept reel for allawi.psd

## Intent

The user's stats (Metricool, 3–7 Oct 2026): reels are the only posts reaching non-followers, but only 8–25% of viewers stay past 3 seconds. So the first 3 seconds carry everything; the rest is a reason to stay, and the end loops back into the start.

- **0–0.8s:** a lit room, the bulb on, «كل بيت عراقي / يعرف هاللحظة..». The light flickers and the grid cuts out.
- **0.8–2.1s:** in the dark only the red signal sign is on. «الوطنية طفت..», then «النت ما طفى.» ignites like neon. Then the sub line, the Asiacell logo and «اشترك بباقة نت». This is the finished ad (`marketing/brand-ads-2026/asiacell.html`), recomposed for 9:16.
- **2.95–8.95s:** the music drops, and the ad splits into its 5 layers in 3D. Four beats walk through them: the bulb (a real photo, cut out and switched off in Photoshop), the neon sign (drawn, not a photo), the text, the logo and button.
- **8.95–11s:** the layers slam back together, and the share CTA «دزّه لواحد بيتهم بلا وطنية هسه».
- **11–12s:** the power comes back, and the frame returns to the opening frame, so the reel loops without a seam.

## Assets

- Bulb: the CC0 photo by Ashesh Magar. The off version comes from `brand-ads-2026/photo/bulb.py`, and the on version and plain crop from `bulb_on.py`.
- Asiacell logo from asiacell.com (brand red #e82228).
- Music: "Gummies" (Mixkit Stock Music Free License), the same bed as `hf-allawi-promo`.
- SFX: the room hum, flicker, power cut, neon and power back are synthesized by `sfx.py`. The kicks, whooshes and pops are the user's own kit, copied from `hf-allawi-promo`.
