# Brief — Asiacell concept reel for allawi.psd

## Intent

v2. The user said v1 (12 s, with a 3D layer breakdown) was "meh": the start looked fake and the middle was boring. They chose the short punchy ad. v3: they liked v2 but found it too fast for the ad to land, and didn't want the «دزّه لواحد بيتهم بلا وطنية هسه» line. The pacing is slower, the full ad holds for about 5.5 s, and the share line is gone.

The user's stats (Metricool, 3–7 Oct 2026): reels are the only posts reaching non-followers, but only 8–25% of viewers stay past 3 seconds. So the joke lands by 2.4 s and the brand by 3.4 s, and the reel stays under 10 s so it gets watched to the end and replayed.

- **0–1.12s:** the real room (the CC0 bulb photo) lit, with «كل بيت عراقي / يعرف هاللحظة..». The grid flickers and drops.
- **1.12–1.9s:** black, the filament's afterglow, and «الوطنية طفت..».
- **1.95–2.9s:** «النت» (white neon) and «ما طفى.» (red neon) ignite, and their red light spills over the room. The signal bars light up, and the street generator starts.
- **3.1–8.8s:** the sub line, the logo and the button, then the small «تصميم مقترح · @allawi.psd». The full ad holds.
- **8.8–9.9s:** the power comes back and the frame returns to the opening frame, so the reel loops.

## Assets

- Room and bulb: the CC0 photo by Ashesh Magar, graded into lit, dark and red-lit plates by `photo.py`.
- Asiacell logo from asiacell.com (brand red #e82228).
- SFX: synthesized by `sfx.py`, plus the user's own kit (boom, whoosh, pops). No music.
