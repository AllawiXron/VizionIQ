## Intent

A sales reel for @allawi.psd, the user's design page. The user wants it strong enough that business owners actually
DM him "I want 3 posts" or "5 posts": "make it so good that I will actually get messages … great selling, go all out".

Chosen concept (pitch #4 with #1's ending): the reel IS an Instagram DM. A shop owner writes «هلو، أريد ٣ بوستات
لمطعمي»; Ali answers and sends samples of his work as image messages; the owner reacts; a receipt prints the order with
its price. It ends on quick-reply buttons «٣ بوستات» / «٥ بوستات» / «باقة شهرية» and «دزّلي وحدة منهن», with the
send button pulsing. The point: show the exact message to send, show ordering is easy, show the real work.
