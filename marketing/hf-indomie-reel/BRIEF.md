# Brief — Indomie concept reel «آخر الشهر» for allawi.psd

## Intent

The user wants likes, not clients, for now. From five pitched concepts, they picked «آخر الشهر»: a salary drains from 750,000 to 2,250 دينار over the month, then the Indomie lands. It is relatable humour, made for video from the start (not a converted post).

- **0–1.5s:** a banking app on salary day, «750,000 د.ع» with a cha-ching, and the hook «راتبي من يوم ١.. ليوم ٢٨».
- **1.5–5.25s:** one expense per beat of the music. The date jumps forward and the balance runs down: الإيجار، المولدة، سوق البيت، قسط الموبايل، غدا ويه الشباب، باقة نت، هدية، كارت رصيد، then «ما أدري وين راحت».
- **5.47s:** a record scratch and the music stops. «يوم ٢٨..», the balance turns red at 2,250, sad trombone, then «شيجيب 2,250 دينار؟».
- **7.2–10.35s:** a whip to the real plate of Indomie with eggs (CC0 photo) and the music back. «الراتب يخلص.. / الاندومي ما يخلص.», the sticker «2,250 دينار = عشا ملكي 👑», the Indomie logo and «جاهز بـ٣ دقايق».
- **10.35–11s:** next month's salary notification lands and the app comes back exactly as it opened, so the reel loops.

Lessons from the Asiacell reel, applied here:
- a real look (a native-feeling app screen and a real photo);
- the ad holds for about 3 s;
- no share line;
- textcheck on every text state.
