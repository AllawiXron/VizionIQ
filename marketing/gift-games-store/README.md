# Gift card for a new games store (10 Oct 2026)

A DM lead (replied to an ad; shop @mer.sto2) is opening a new games store and wants a new identity and logo. The user
chose to send him a discount gift: the full identity at 120 ألف instead of 150 (خصم 30 ألف), valid 7 days, until
17 تشرين الأول 2026.

`out/gift.jpg` (1620×2025, 4:5) shows it as a game gift card, the kind of product his store sells. The card has the
allawi.psd wordmark, «GIFT CARD», a gamepad icon and «خصم 30 ألف». The scratch-off code NEWSTORE30 is half scratched.
A hologram sticker reads «VALID 7 DAYS». The card is taped to the allawi.psd paper. Below it are the price
(«الهوية الكاملة» 150 struck through, 120 ألف) and what's included (the logo, colours and fonts, highlight covers,
post and story templates). A rubber stamp reads «صالحة لغاية ١٧ تشرين الأول», with the CTA «ردّ بـ «تمام» ونبدي».

Brand colours and fonts are from `../ig-highlights`: paper, ink #1f1510, orange #e2541b / #f07a2e, Alexandria,
Aref Ruqaa, IBM Plex Sans Arabic and Courier Prime. Render it with `node render.cjs`.
