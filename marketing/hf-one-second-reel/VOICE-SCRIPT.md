## Recording tips

- Record on your phone in a small room with soft things around (a wardrobe or a car works). Hold the phone about a
  hand's length from your mouth, a little to the side.
- Read the whole thing 2–3 times in a row, then keep the best take.
- Small pauses at the «..» are good: they give each line room to land.
- Send me the voice file (any format), and I'll fit it to the video: I can move each scene to land on your words, put
  the sound effects under your voice, and clean the voice (noise, level). Or drop it in CapCut over
  `renders/one-second-reel.mp4` yourself: the sound effects are already low so your voice stays on top.
