## Intent

The user's third sales reel for @allawi.psd (15 Oct). He sent a reference reel: a designer's 68 s voice-led reel
(flat blue / cream / black fields, word-by-word captions with one highlighted word, packaging mockups popping in and
stacking, a phone mockup, a "follow me" pill at the end). His ask: "make a video idea like this but … for the viewers
to come order and come dm me … dont make it 100% same idea … make it better and shorter maybe like 30 sec … i will add
voice over by myself".

What we keep from the reference: a voice that teaches one idea, clean flat colour fields, word-by-word captions with a
highlighted word, designs popping in and stacking. What we change, and why it should work better:

- **The problem shown, not described.** A one-second bar fills; a phone feed flies past, then the scroll stops dead on
  his design.
- **Proof, not opinion.** The three things that stop a thumb (one idea, the customer's dialect, a reason to order now)
  build up as a list, each next to one of his own designs, with the key phrase selected.
- **What he makes, not what it costs.** v3–v7 said his bundle price out loud («٣ بوستات بـ٤٠ ألف»); in v8 the user
  asked for no prices, so the scene lists his services from his price highlight instead (بوستات، ستوريات، منيو، شعار،
  وهوية كاملة), and the caption lists them too.
- **A DM ask, not "follow me".** «عجبك الشغل؟ راسلني بالدايركت.. ونبدي بمشروعك.» (v3 asked for a keyword, «بوست»;
  the user preferred a plain "DM me").
- **38 s instead of 68 s,** at the reference's calm pace (v2 at 30 s was "rushed"; v3 at 45 s, the user asked for 38).

## Look (v4: his highlights, not "Claude motion")

v2–v3 used flat cream / orange / near-black fields; the user said the colours were not his and looked like "Claude
motion", and pointed at his highlights (`../ig-highlights`). v4 uses the highlights' exact recipe: paper `#ece3d4`,
brown `#1f1510` and the orange gradient, each under the paper texture (multiply), film grain and a vignette, which renders
as greige `#d5c9b5`, warm brown `#2e231c` and rust `#c8501b`; Alexandria 800/900 on torn cream strips; his designs as
taped prints with kraft tags; Aref Ruqaa for the handwritten list and notes; an orange highlighter and marker ticks.
The motion stays the reference's: words out of a blur, whip-pans, circle reveals, zoom-throughs, sideways slams.
v7–v8 set every word, the handwriting included, in his own DG Sahabah.

## One take (v5)

The user asked for the video "one-take": no cuts. Everything sits on one desk and a single camera travels across
it (pans, a push-in, a pull-back over a wall of all his work, a dive), with light motion blur on the moves. v5 was
"bad and rushed in some clips"; v6 never lands on an empty frame and moves less, slower and softer (README).

## Sound

Sound effects only from the user's pack (`sfx.sh`). No music (CLAUDE.md: ask the user for a track if a piece needs
one). The effects sit low so his voice leads.
